הצהרת נגישות

הצהרה זו חלה על אתר שוקולדות – אירית שמעוני.

Our Commitment

Irit Shimoni Chocolates is committed to ensuring our services are accessible to people with disabilities. We aim to conform with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This accessibility statement forms an integral part of our Terms of Use.

Accessibility Features

You should be able to:

  • Zoom in up to 200% without loss of information or functionality
  • All decorative images are hidden from screen readers
  • Page uses a logical heading structure (H1 → H2)
  • Interactive elements include accessible ARIA labels
  • Change colors, contrast levels, and fonts
  • Navigate the website with just a keyboard
  • Listen to the website using a screen reader
  • Stop moving images

Compatibility with Assistive Technology

Our website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Mobile Accessibility Features: AssistiveTouch (iOS)
  • Screen Magnification: Magnification (Android), Magnifier (Windows) and Zoom (macOS/iOS)
  • Screen Readers: NVDA (Windows), JAWS (Windows) and VoiceOver (macOS/iOS)
  • Voice Recognition & Control: Dragon NaturallySpeaking (Windows/Mac)

Conformance Status

Status: Partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA

We conducted our most recent accessibility evaluation on 30/03/2026.

Non-Accessible Content

We are actively working to address any accessibility issues as part of an ongoing plan to improve accessibility. We will update this statement when limitations are identified and resolved.

Israeli Accessibility Standard IS 5568

This website is built in compliance with Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 Level AA. IS 5568 is the official Israeli web accessibility standard mandated under the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law. We are committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all users, including people with disabilities, in accordance with Israeli law.

Preparation of This Statement

This statement was prepared on 30 March 2026 and is reviewed annually or when we make significant service changes.

We maintain records of accessibility feedback and our responses for regulatory compliance.

How We Test

We evaluate accessibility using:

  • Self-assessment conducted in accordance with EAA requirements
  • Ongoing monitoring and regular accessibility reviews
  • Evaluated against Israeli Standard IS 5568 (based on WCAG 2.0 AA)
